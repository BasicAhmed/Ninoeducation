Formal tone for the apply flow, new cost-and-payment-methods guide, header made genuinely transparent

1. Rewrote every casual/slang string in the apply wizard and thank-you
   page (both Arabic and English) to a formal, professional register.
   The apply flow had been deliberately built in casual Gulf Arabic
   slang throughout ('shu', 'wain', 'yalla', 'khalna', etc., plus the
   English equivalents like "Let's launch it! 🚀") which read as
   unprofessional for what is, functionally, an official application
   form. Rewrote roughly 70 strings across apply, applyPage, and
   thankYou namespaces in both languages to proper MSA / professional
   English while keeping the aviation-themed language that IS
   appropriate branding (boarding pass, captain, destination) — the
   fix targets the casual grammar and slang, not the aviation
   metaphor itself.

2. New guide at /guides/why-is-flight-training-expensive answering
   exactly what was asked: why training costs what it does (real
   operational cost drivers — fuel, maintenance, insurance,
   instructor certification, regulatory compliance — not arbitrary
   pricing), whether full payment upfront is required (no), and a
   full explanation of both payment methods flight schools actually
   use: pay-as-you-go (lower upfront commitment, pay only for hours
   used, needs ongoing budget discipline) versus fixed packages
   (predictable total cost, usually discounted, needs more certainty
   before committing). Added to lib/guides.ts, the sitemap, and
   cross-linked both directions with the existing detailed cost guide
   so they read as complementary rather than redundant.

3. Fixed header transparency for real — the previous frosted style
   (white/70 + blur) was technically alpha-transparent in code, but
   perceptually indistinguishable from a solid header on the many
   inner pages with flat cream/white backgrounds, since there's
   nothing visually different behind it to reveal. Reduced to white/25
   with a lighter blur — confirmed via screenshot that the photo
   hero's wing detail is now genuinely visible through the header
   while text stays legible, and confirmed inner pages now blend
   seamlessly with no visible header-shaped box sitting on top.

Verified via Playwright, and this surfaced a real lesson worth
recording: an early test run appeared to show the apply wizard
completely broken (Next button not advancing steps at all, ghosted
overlapping panel content). Rather than assume the formal-tone rewrite
had broken something and start reverting, traced it down properly —
checked element bounding boxes, found the button's calculated Y
position was negative (off-screen), checked window.scrollY, and
confirmed with a direct DOM click() (bypassing Playwright's
coordinate-based synthetic mouse click) that the wizard actually
advances steps correctly and every rewritten string displays exactly
as intended. The failure was an artifact of Playwright's
auto-scroll-before-click interacting with this page's smooth-scroll
CSS in this sandboxed environment, not a real application bug —
confirmed by reproducing correct behavior through multiple wizard
steps via direct clicks before concluding the rewrite was safe to ship.
