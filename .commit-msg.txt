New page: /scholarships — targets aspiring pilots who assume aviation training is unaffordable and never reach out

Ahmed's insight: people who dream of flying but see the price tag and
self-select out entirely are the biggest segment of the market, and
the site currently has nothing that speaks to them directly — the
cost guide honestly explains why training is expensive, which is the
right call, but leaves that reader with nowhere hopeful to go next.

Built the page around real, honest alternative paths rather than
vague inspiration: scholarships (framed accurately as opportunities
that come up periodically and aren't always publicly advertised, not
guaranteed or currently-available-to-everyone, which would be
dishonest), school discounts on specific intakes, starting with a
much smaller step (a trial flight, a workshop, PPL alone instead of
the full program), and low-cost introductory workshops with real
pilots. The CTA is deliberately a low-commitment 'register your
interest' via WhatsApp rather than pushing straight into the full
apply flow, since someone in this position isn't ready to commit a
budget yet — they're ready to be kept in the loop.

Full SEO structure: Article + FAQPage JSON-LD (same pattern as the
guides), canonical URL, added to the sitemap at a higher priority and
weekly changeFrequency given the size of the audience this targets,
and added to llms.txt for AI-system discoverability, matching the
existing pattern for every other core page.

Cross-linked deliberately, not just added as an isolated page: added
a dedicated callout section directly inside the 'why is flight
training expensive' guide — exactly the reader who just found out the
real cost and might otherwise leave — plus its own related-guides
entry, and a footer link so it's reachable from anywhere on the site.

Verified via Playwright: confirmed the page loads with two valid JSON-LD
schemas, confirmed the WhatsApp registration link resolves to the real
business number with the exact intended human, non-pushy message text
(decoded and read directly, not assumed), confirmed the FAQ accordion
actually expands on click rather than just rendering closed, and
confirmed the new callout section in the cost guide renders correctly
and links through to this page.
